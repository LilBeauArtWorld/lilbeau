🍰 Baking & Sweets Selection

Turkish Yoghurt Cake

I made this cake for my grandfather when he turned 83. My mother sent me the New York Times recipe, and because of my Turkish family background I really wanted to try it. It was moist, lemony, soft and fluffy, with powdered sugar on top. I definitely want to make it again.

Red Velvet Cake

This is one of my favorite birthday cakes. I usually use a box mix for the cake, but I make the frosting myself. I have made this cake for many birthdays.
