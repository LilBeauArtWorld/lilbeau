Currently Reading & Favorites

A Court of Thorns and Roses

Sarah J. Maas

★★★★☆

I loved reading this on vacation in Spain. I read halfway through it in about an hour and my mother told me to calm down. The world-building was amazing and I loved the strong female protagonist. I give it 4 stars because the later books are even better.

The Love Hypothesis

Ali Hazelwood

★★★★★

One of my favorites. It is a short and fun read with the fake-dating trope. The male love interest is Dutch, which made me laugh because apparently Dutch men are everywhere.

The Deal

Elle Kennedy

★★★★★

There is a series and an adaptation. The adaptation did well, but it left some things out. The characters are mostly green flags, and it is the perfect rainy and cozy read.

Keep it cozy, keep it simple, keep it you. ♡
