Currently Reading & Favorites

A Court of Thorn and Roses

By Sarah J. Maas

⭐⭐⭐⭐

I absolutely loved this book when I first read it on vacation to spain. I remember being halfway through the book after just one hour. Even my mother told me to calm down or else there wasn't anything else to read. It has a great world building, I love the strong female protagonist. Amaze Amaze! The only reason I give it a 4-star, is because the books after this one, are ven better.

The Love Hypothesis

By Ali Hazelwood

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

My favourite! i abosolutely adore this book as well as the writer! It's a short book (in my opinion), but it drags you into it so well! Also, I'm a sucker for the 'fake-dating' trope in where the guys just folds almost immediatly inside his mind. By the way, have I told you the male love interest is Dutch? Makes me wonder where guys like him are in the Netherlands...

The Deal

By Elle Kennedy

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

I know, I know... There is a series about this. Well if you liked that, I definitely recommend you to read this book. teh adaptation did an absolute amazing job, but of course there will be important stuff left out. I love the fact that despite the drama, almost every character in this universe is mostly a green flag. It's a really good book to just enjoy when it rains outside. Easy to read and just lovely.

Keep it cozy, keep it simple, keep it you. ♡
