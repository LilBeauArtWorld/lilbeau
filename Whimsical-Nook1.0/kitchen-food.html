🍰 Baking & Sweets Selection

Turkish Yoghurt Cake

I have recently made this delicious Turkisch yoghurt cake for my grandfather who had turned 83 years old. Happy birthday to him! My mother saw a recipe from New York Times and sended it to me on a random night. I didn't think much of it, until my brain literally started to click like: "Hey wait a second... My grandfather came from Turkey with my grandmother and my mom... Why shouldn't bake this for him?" So I did. And it was a success! The cake was moist, a hint of lemon-ish, soft and fluffy. Some powdered sugar on top made it even more delicious. I will definitely make this cake again in the future.

Red Velvet Cake

My god yes! My to go choice of birthday cake if I have to choose something chocolat-y. I do have a confession to make... I have never made it from scratch, but only with a box mix for the cake part... I do make the frosting from scratch though! I have made this type of cake for many birthdays by now, and it never gets old or tiring. I love it and everyone should love it... Unless you have allergies of course...
