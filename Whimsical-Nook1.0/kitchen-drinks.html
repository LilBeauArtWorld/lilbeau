🍹 Botanical Liquid Spirits

Aperol Spritz

My f*cking god yes, this is the drink to go. Especially on hot days, but you still want to get wasted (slowly). This an bitter orange juice, super easy to make so of course have I made this at home for friends and family before. plus, you can make it as light or as strong as you want. The nice thing about spritzes, is that there are many variations of it. You can make it with Aperol, Campari, or even with elderflower liqueur. I even have made it with limoncello, so if you so happen to be allergic to oranges, you won't be missing out, because Spritz doesn't discriminate.

Espresso Martini

Piña Colada

A classic tropical drink. I remember it being one of my first cocktails that I have ordered at a restaurant when I turned 18 years old (yes you can legally drink at that age in The Netherlands). Also this lactose free... so lactose intolerant people, you have no excuse not to not try this out if you haven't already (unless you are allergic to pineapple or alcohol, or cocunut).
