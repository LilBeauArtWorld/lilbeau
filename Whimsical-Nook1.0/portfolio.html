Product Designs

Matcha Cat Bag

A cozy tote bag design featuring a whimsical cat with matcha/coffee colours. I made this for an acquintance of mine who loves to drink matcha and has two cats.

Mother's Working Bag

I made this for my mother, who always uses plastic bags to carry her food in. I thought to myself, mother's day is coming up... I don't have a gift yet... I will make one. Felt kind of childish to 'make' a gift again, instead of just buying it. It was really nice tho and she enjoyed it very much.

My Illustration Work

Characters Design #1

I was screwing around with some desert themed subjects. I know from myself that I mostly make characters that live in just the normal modern cities. I gave myself a small challenge to make like a fantasy-dragon-like-prince-person who lives in a desert. It was a fun challenge.

Characters Design #2

Most of the times I draw my characters by themselves, in duo's or only form the torso and up. So I wanted to challenge myself and put them into a group selfie picture. I found it difficult, but it was rewarding and fun to see them all together like that.

Characters Design #3

Hehehe, I remember making this while having to study for a big test. So of course, me being me, I didn't study and instead made a drawing of me struggling to study while one of my own characters helps me out.

Spoiler alert: I failed the test...
